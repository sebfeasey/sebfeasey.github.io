About

Hi, I’m Seb!

I’m currently completing my Bachelor’s Degree in Graphic Design (DNMADe Graphisme, Spécialité édition) at the Lycée de la Communication in Metz, France.

Having known from as soon as middle school that I wanted to build a career in this domain, I’ve been able to develop my style and find the things I really like over the years. Still loooooooooads more of evolution for me to go through of course, I’m looking forward to it!

With a love for bright bold colours and funky typography, I enjoy creating whether it's a publication, poster or visual identity and I feel comfortable working with pretty much any type of style or vibe that gets chucked at me.

This website is still a work in progress at the moment, but this space will be dedicated to sharing my projects, experiments and creative journey as I continue to learn and grow as a designer.
